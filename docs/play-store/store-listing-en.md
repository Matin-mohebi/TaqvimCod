## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
Taqvim is a complete, fast and offline Persian (Jalali) calendar for Android. Get the Solar Hijri, Gregorian and Lunar Hijri calendars in one beautiful app — with Iranian holidays and events, prayer times, qibla compass, two-way Google Calendar sync, and handy daily tools.

📅 FULL JALALI CALENDAR
Browse the solar Hijri month with Persian digits, a highlighted today marker, smooth month and year navigation, and official Iranian holidays for every day. Dates are computed with an accurate Jalali algorithm, valid for years far into the past and future.

🕌 PRAYER TIMES & QIBLA
See imsak, sunrise, sunset and the five daily prayers for your city. Pick your city from a built-in list of Iranian cities or enter coordinates manually — everything is computed on-device. The qibla compass uses your phone's sensors to point to Mecca and shows the qibla angle from true north.

🔔 PRAYER REMINDERS
Optional local-notification reminders for prayer times. No server, no internet.

🤝 TWO-WAY GOOGLE CALENDAR SYNC
See your Google Calendar events inside Taqvim, and create Persian-calendar events directly in Google Calendar. Changes sync both ways instantly through the system calendar provider — the app itself never sends data anywhere.

✅ DAILY PLANNER
Build a to-do list for each day, check items off, and watch your daily progress. Every day keeps its own list; data stays in the app's private storage.

🌍 WORLD CALENDAR & CLOCKS
A monthly Gregorian view with Jalali and Hijri equivalents, live clocks for Tehran, London, New York and Tokyo, plus a 45-day list of upcoming holidays and events with countdowns.

🔎 EVENT SEARCH
Search this month's events by title, description or location, and jump straight to the event's date from any result.

🧮 CALENDAR TOOLS
Convert dates across Jalali, Gregorian and Hijri; calculate the distance between two dates; countdown to Nowruz; and compute an exact age in years, months and days — all offline.

📊 HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
A calendar widget for today's date and a prayer-times widget for today's schedule, without opening the app.

🎨 THREE THEMES
Light, dark and auto (follows system), with a full right-to-left layout and the Vazirmatn font.

🔒 REAL PRIVACY
This app has NO internet permission. Nothing is collected, transmitted or shared; no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Settings are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on device. The source code is open on GitHub.

Install Taqvim now and stay on top of 1405 and every year after.
```
